Meet the Eye Behind Beast Visuals

Get to Know Me Beyond the Camera

I am a passionate creative who finds purpose in capturing and telling stories through different mediums. My skills span across photography, video editing, graphic design, and audio-visual production, where I currently serve as the media lead at my church. Beyond the technical side, I love expressing myself through poetry and content creation that carries meaning and impact.

Outside the creative space, I have a strong love for fitness—whether it’s rugby, running, or staying active—and I find joy in building discipline through it. I’m deeply committed to youth development and spend much of my time uplifting and guiding young people, using both creativity and mentorship to inspire the next generation.”

Frequently Asked Questions

FAQ

Find quick answers to the most common questions

How far in advance should I book?

For events or weddings, we recommend booking at least 4–6 weeks in advance. For portrait or content shoots, 1–2 weeks is usually enough — depending on our schedule.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes, we do. A deposit is needed to lock in your booking. This helps us plan and prepare for your shoot with confidence. The balance can be settled on or before your session.

What happens if it rains or I need to reschedule?

We’re flexible! You can reschedule once at no extra cost, provided you give at least 48 hours’ notice. For outdoor shoots affected by weather, we’ll work together to find a new date or location.

How long does it take to receive my photos?

Editing typically takes 7–14 days, depending on the type of session and number of photos. For larger events or weddings, it may take a bit longer — but we’ll keep you updated.

How will I receive my photos?

Your final edited images will be delivered digitally through a secure online gallery or download link. Prints and USB delivery options are also available upon request.

Do you travel for shoots?

Yes! We’re happy to travel for destination shoots or events. Travel fees may apply depending on the distance and location.

Can you help with creative direction or posing?

Absolutely. We guide you throughout the shoot — from poses and angles to mood and energy — so you can feel confident and relaxed in front of the camera.
